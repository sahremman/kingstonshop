About Kingston's Business

Learn more about our journey, mission, and commitment to excellence

Our Story

Kingston's Business was founded in 2015 with a simple vision: to provide the local community with high-quality products and exceptional service at fair prices. What began as a small electronics repair shop has grown into a comprehensive retail establishment serving the diverse needs of Gardnesville Township.

Our founder, Kingston Smith, recognized the need for a reliable source of electronics and everyday essentials in the community. Drawing on his background in electronics and passion for customer service, he established a business that would prioritize customer satisfaction and community engagement.

Over the years, we've expanded our product offerings to include groceries, household items, and various accessories, becoming a true one-stop shop for our customers. Despite our growth, we remain committed to the personal touch and community focus that have been our hallmarks since day one.
