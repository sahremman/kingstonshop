Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you deliver to?

We currently offer delivery services within Gardnesville Township and surrounding areas within a 15-mile radius. For locations outside this area, please contact our customer support team for special arrangements.

How long does a typical electronic repair take?

Most minor repairs are completed within 24-48 hours. More complex repairs may take 3-5 business days. We'll provide you with an estimated timeframe when you drop off your device.

Do you charge for device diagnostics?

No, we offer free diagnostics for all electronic devices. You'll only be charged if you approve the repair after receiving our diagnosis and quote.

How can I track my custom order?

Once your custom order is processed, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number on our website or contact our customer support team for updates on your order status.

What is covered under your product warranty?

Our standard warranty covers manufacturing defects and malfunctions not caused by misuse. The warranty period varies by product category: electronics (1 year), appliances (2 years), and accessories (6 months). Extended warranty options are available at the time of purchase.
