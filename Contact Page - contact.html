Frequently Asked Questions

What are your business hours?

We are open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and on Sundays from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Do you offer product delivery?

Yes, we offer same-day delivery for orders placed before 2:00 PM within Gardnesville Township. For areas outside our immediate vicinity, delivery may take 1-2 business days.

How can I check the status of my repair?

You can check your repair status by calling our customer service line at +231 777 344 868 or by visiting our store with your repair receipt. We also send SMS updates for major repair milestones.

Do you accept returns or exchanges?

Yes, we accept returns and exchanges within 14 days of purchase for most products, provided they are in their original condition with packaging and receipt. Some electronics and perishable items may have different return policies.
